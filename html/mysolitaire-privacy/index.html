Overview

Chengyuan Solitaire ("the App") is developed and published by studyplace. It is an offline solitaire game that runs entirely on your device. The App has no accounts, no sign-in, and never uploads your game data to any server.

Your settings and saved games never leave this device.

The only part that connects to the internet is the built-in banner ad. This page explains what the App and that ad service each handle.

What the App collects

The App itself collects, transmits and shares no personal data. There is no analytics, no crash reporting, no user tracking and no in-app purchase.

Data that stays on your device

The following is stored in the App's private directory on your device:

  • Preferences: interface language, text size, sound and vibration toggles, card colours, high-contrast mode
  • Saved progress for each solitaire game
  • Whether you have seen each game's tutorial

Uninstalling the App deletes all of it.

Third-party advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows banner ads to support continued development. Ads are served by Google AdMob, which collects and uses the following on your device:

Data typePurpose
Advertising IDServing ads, measuring performance, limiting repeated ads
Device and diagnostic informationAd delivery and fraud prevention
Approximate location (inferred from IP)Serving locally relevant ads

Your game data and settings are never used for advertising, and are never shared with Google or any third party. The ad SDK cannot read the App's saved data.

For how Google uses data, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Users in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland

The App uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to ask for your consent to personalised advertising the first time you open it. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad Privacy Settings. That entry appears only where consent is required — elsewhere Google reports that no consent form is needed and none is shown.

Permissions the App requests

PermissionWhy
Internet, network stateTo fetch ads from Google. The game itself is fully offline and plays without a connection.
VibrateHaptic feedback on moves. Can be turned off in Settings.
Advertising IDUsed by the Google Mobile Ads SDK for ad delivery and measurement.

Your choices

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads. Deleting it stops personalised ads.
  • Turn off vibration in the App's Settings.
  • Change your advertising consent (where applicable) in Settings → Ad Privacy Settings.
  • Delete local data by uninstalling the App or clearing its data in system settings.

Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal data, please contact us at the address below.

Retention and international transfers

The developer retains no user data. Ad-related data is retained and processed by Google under its own policies, and may be transferred to and stored on servers outside your country or region.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, this page and the "last updated" date above are updated.
