Overview

MeowBeat: Cat Rhythm Hop ("the App", package com.cheng.yuan.meowbeat) is developed and published by studyplace. It is a single-player music rhythm game that runs entirely on your device. The App has no accounts, no sign-in, and never uploads your game data to any server.

Your progress and settings never leave this device.

The only parts that use the internet are the banner ad and the optional "watch an ad to revive" reward video. This page explains what the App and that ad service each handle.

What the App collects

The App itself collects, transmits and shares no personal data. There is no analytics SDK, no crash reporting, no user tracking, and no in-app purchase — every cat and trail effect is unlocked with coins earned by playing.

Data that stays on your device

The following is stored in the App's private directory:

  • Best score, grade, stars and completion for each song and difficulty
  • Coins, the cats and trail effects you own, and which ones are equipped
  • Settings: interface language, music / cat / effects volume, 3D quality, reduced-effects mode
  • The automatic audio-sync offset measured on your device

Uninstalling the App deletes all of it. Clearing the app's data in Android settings has the same effect.

Third-party advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows a banner ad, and a rewarded video you may choose to watch to revive a failed run. Ads are served by Google AdMob, which collects and uses the following on your device:

Data typePurpose
Advertising IDServing ads, measuring performance, limiting repeated ads
Device and diagnostic informationAd delivery and fraud prevention
Approximate location (inferred from IP)Serving locally relevant ads

Your scores, coins and settings are never used for advertising, and are never shared with Google or any third party. The ad SDK cannot read the App's saved data.

For how Google uses data, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Users in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland

The App uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to ask for your consent to personalised advertising the first time you open it. You can change or withdraw that choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options, reachable from the song menu and from the pause menu. That entry appears only where consent is required — elsewhere Google reports that no consent form is needed and none is shown.

Permissions the App requests

PermissionWhy
Internet, network stateTo fetch ads from Google. The game itself plays fully offline.
Advertising IDUsed by the Google Mobile Ads SDK for ad delivery and measurement.
Read basic phone stateRequested by the Google Mobile Ads SDK. The App never reads your phone number or any subscriber identity.
Wake lock, foreground serviceRequested by the ads and game engine components so playback and ad loading are not interrupted.

The App requests no access to location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or files.

Your choices

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads. Deleting it stops personalised ads.
  • Change your advertising consent (where applicable) in the App's Settings → Privacy options.
  • Delete local data by uninstalling the App, or by clearing its data in Android Settings → Apps.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child's data has been collected, contact us and we will address it promptly.

Data retention and deletion

We hold no data about you, so there are no server records to delete. On-device data is under your control as described above. Data handled by Google AdMob is retained under Google's own policies.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page is updated and the revised policy ships with the corresponding App release.

인터넷, 네트워크 상태Google에서 광고를 받아오기 위해서입니다. 게임 자체는 오프라인으로도 즐길 수 있습니다.
광고 IDGoogle 모바일 광고 SDK가 게재와 측정에 사용합니다.
기본 전화 상태 읽기Google 모바일 광고 SDK의 요구 사항입니다. 전화번호나 가입자 식별 정보를 읽지 않습니다.
웨이크 락, 포그라운드 서비스광고와 게임 엔진 구성 요소가 요청합니다. 재생과 광고 로딩이 끊기지 않도록 하기 위해서입니다.

위치, 카메라, 마이크, 연락처, 사진, 파일 접근 권한은 요청하지 않습니다.

선택할 수 있는 것

  • 광고 ID 재설정 또는 삭제: Android 설정 → 개인정보 보호 → 광고. 삭제하면 맞춤 광고가 중단됩니다.
  • 광고 동의 변경(해당 지역): 본 앱의 설정 → 개인정보 설정.
  • 기기 내 데이터 삭제: 앱을 삭제하거나 Android 설정 → 앱에서 데이터를 지우세요.

아동

본 앱은 만 13세 미만 아동을 대상으로 하지 않으며, 의도적으로 데이터를 수집하지 않습니다. 아동의 데이터가 수집되었다고 생각되면 연락해 주세요. 신속히 처리하겠습니다.
