概述

單純記帳本（以下稱「本 App」）由 studyplace 開發與發行， 是一款在裝置本機運作的收支記帳工具。 本 App 沒有帳號系統、不需要登入、也不會把你的記帳內容上傳到任何伺服器。 你輸入的每一筆收入與支出都只存在你自己的手機裡。

你的記帳資料不會離開這台裝置。

唯一會連上網路的部分是內建的橫幅廣告。本頁說明本 App 與該廣告服務各自處理哪些資料。

本 App 收集的資料

本 App 本身不收集、不傳送、也不分享任何個人資料。 沒有分析工具、沒有當機回報服務、沒有使用者追蹤。

你在 App 內建立的內容（金額、日期、分類、備忘）僅儲存在裝置本機。

第三方廣告（Google AdMob）

本 App 顯示橫幅廣告以支持後續開發。廣告由 Google AdMob 提供， 該服務會在你的裝置上收集與使用下列資料：

資料類型用途
廣告識別碼（Advertising ID）投放廣告、衡量成效、避免重複顯示同一則廣告
裝置與診斷資訊廣告投放與詐騙防範
粗略位置（依 IP 推估）投放符合當地的廣告

你的收支紀錄不會被用於廣告用途，也不會提供給 Google 或任何第三方。 廣告 SDK 讀取不到本 App 的資料庫內容。

Google 如何在其服務中使用資料，請見 Google 的合作夥伴網站資料使用說明。

歐洲經濟區、英國與瑞士的使用者

本 App 使用 Google 的使用者訊息平台（UMP）在首次啟動時徵求你對個人化廣告的同意。 你可以隨時在 設定 → 廣告隱私設定 → 變更廣告同意設定 重新選擇。

你的資料存在哪裡

  • 記帳資料儲存在裝置本機的 SQLite 資料庫，並以 SQLCipher 加密。
  • 加密金鑰由 Android Keystore 保管，不會離開裝置。
  • 本 App 不使用雲端同步，也沒有伺服器端的資料備份。
  • 若你啟用安全鎖，數字密碼會以加鹽雜湊（salted hash）形式儲存，不會存明碼。 指紋與生物辨識由 Android 系統處理，本 App 取得的只有「驗證成功或失敗」的結果， 拿不到也不會儲存你的生物特徵資料。

Overview

Simple Ledger (the “app”) is developed and published by studyplace. It is an income and expense tracker that runs entirely on your device. The app has no account system, requires no sign-in, and never uploads your records to any server. Every entry you create stays on your own phone.

Your ledger data never leaves this device.

The only part of the app that connects to the internet is the built-in banner ad. This page explains what the app and that advertising service each handle.

What the app collects

The app itself collects, transmits and shares nothing. There is no analytics, no crash reporting service, and no user tracking.

The content you create in the app (amounts, dates, categories, memos) is stored only on your device.

Third-party advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows a banner ad to support ongoing development. Ads are served by Google AdMob, which collects and uses the following on your device:

Data typePurpose
Advertising IDServing ads, measuring performance, frequency capping
Device and diagnostic informationAd delivery and fraud prevention
Approximate location (derived from IP)Serving regionally relevant ads

Your income and expense records are never used for advertising and are never shared with Google or any third party. The advertising SDK has no access to the app’s database.

For how Google uses data from its services, see Google’s partner sites data policy.

Users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

The app uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to ask for your consent to personalised advertising on first launch. You can change your choice at any time under Settings → Ad privacy → Change ad consent.

Where your data lives

  • Records are stored in a local SQLite database, encrypted with SQLCipher.
  • The encryption key is held by the Android Keystore and never leaves the device.
  • There is no cloud sync and no server-side backup.
  • If you enable the security lock, your PIN is stored as a salted hash, never in plain text. Fingerprint and biometric checks are handled by Android; the app only receives a success or failure result and never obtains or stores your biometric data.

Backup files

You can export a JSON backup to a location you choose. Backup files are not encrypted — they are readable plain text. Once exported, the file is yours to look after; the app cannot track or delete it. Avoid storing it somewhere you do not trust.

Deleting your data

All data is local, so uninstalling the app deletes it. You can also delete individual records and categories inside the app. Because studyplace holds none of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on your behalf and no request is needed.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.
